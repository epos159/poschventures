Delete Your Data

proseuchē · Last updated: July 2026

Data stored on your device

proseuchē stores your prayer lists, praises, Bible notes, archive, and settings only on your device. Posch Ventures does not operate servers that store your prayer data. You can remove everything the app controls using the steps below.

How to delete data in the app

  1. Open proseuchē on your device
  2. Delete individual prayers or entire lists from your home / list screens
  3. If archive is enabled, open Settings → View archived and remove items you no longer want
  4. To remove all local app data at once, uninstall proseuchē from your device

Uninstalling removes local app storage on that device. We do not keep a copy.

Backups you exported

If you used Settings → Export backup and saved a file elsewhere (email, cloud drive, Files app, etc.), delete those backup files in the location where you stored them. Importing a backup replaces your current data on the device.
