Privacy Policy

ToolVault · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

ToolVault is a local-first tool inventory app. Your tools, photos, loan records, and settings are stored on your device. We do not run a cloud account or sync service, and we do not sell your personal information. No ads. No analytics SDKs.

Information We Collect

We do not collect inventory data on our servers. You may enter tool names, quantities, serial numbers, loan details, and photos in the app—all saved locally unless you export, share, or back up files yourself.

Data Storage

Tool lists, drawers, loan records, photos, and backup files stay on your device. We do not have access to this data. Uninstalling the app removes local app data; backup files you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them.

Device Permissions

ToolVault may request camera access (tool photos and barcode scanning), photo library access (choosing existing images), file access (importing backups you select), and notifications (optional loan reminders). You can deny permissions; related features may not work. We do not use the microphone.

Third-Party Services

The app may use Expo updates to deliver bug fixes. If you use Share on a backup or export, your device’s share sheet sends files to services you choose—we do not control those services. If we enable optional text scanning via Google Cloud Vision in the future, selected images may be sent to Google for OCR as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

App Stores

When you download the app from Google Play, Google may collect account and device information as part of their services. That is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@poschventures.com.
