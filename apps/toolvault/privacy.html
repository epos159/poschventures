Privacy Policy

ToolVault · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

ToolVault is a local-first tool inventory app. Your tools, photos, loan records, and settings are stored on your device. We do not run a cloud account or sync service, and we do not sell your personal information. No ads. No analytics SDKs.

Information We Collect

We do not collect inventory data on our servers. You may enter tool names, quantities, serial numbers, loan details, and photos in the app—all saved locally on your device.

Data Storage and Exports

Tool lists, drawers, loan records, photos, and backup files stay on your device. We do not have access to this data. You can export PDF or CSV reports and create local backups when you choose. If you share a backup or export (email, cloud drive, etc.), that transfer uses the service you pick—not our servers. Uninstalling the app removes local app data; files you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them.

Device Permissions

ToolVault may request camera access (tool photos and barcode scanning), photo library access (choosing existing images), file access (backups and exports), and notifications (optional loan reminders). You can deny permissions in device settings; related features may not work.

Updates

The app may use Expo over-the-air updates to deliver bug fixes. When you download from Google Play, Google may collect information according to Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@poschventures.com.
