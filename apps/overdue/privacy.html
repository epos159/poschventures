Privacy Policy

Overdue · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

Overdue is a local-first personal book tracker. Your library, covers, loan records, and settings are stored on your device. We do not run a cloud account or sync service, and we do not sell your personal information. No ads. No analytics SDKs.

Information We Collect

We do not collect your book data on our servers. You may enter titles, authors, ISBNs, reading notes, borrower details, and cover photos in the app—all saved locally on your device.

ISBN Lookup

When you scan or enter an ISBN, the app may send that number to Open Library and Google Books to fetch title, author, and cover details. Those services receive the ISBN you request—not your full library. Their privacy policies govern how they handle that request.

Data Storage and Exports

Book lists, genres, loan records, cover images, and backup files stay on your device. We do not have access to this data. You can export PDF or CSV reports and create local JSON backups when you choose. If you share a backup or export (email, cloud drive, etc.), that transfer uses the service you pick—not our servers. Uninstalling the app removes local app data; files you saved elsewhere remain until you delete them.

Device Permissions

Overdue may request camera access (ISBN scanning and cover photos), photo library access (choosing existing cover images), and file access (backups and exports). You can deny permissions in device settings; related features may not work.

Updates

The app may use Expo over-the-air updates to deliver bug fixes. When you download from Google Play, Google may collect information according to Google's policies.

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The "Last updated" date above will reflect any changes.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@poschventures.com.
